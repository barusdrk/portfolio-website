How it works

From incoming email to reviewed response.

A straightforward workflow that supports automation while keeping customer context and business rules in view.

Frequently asked questions

Before you take a closer look.

Does it work with Gmail and Outlook?

The application includes integrations and email synchronization workflows for Gmail and Outlook.

Does AI send every reply automatically?

No. The workflow supports draft review, approval and escalation. Automatic sending is configurable and should be enabled only with suitable policies and safeguards.

Can it use our company policies?

The knowledge base is designed to organize company support information, including refund, shipping, billing and troubleshooting guidance.

Can I see a demonstration?

Yes. Request a walkthrough and share a little about your support workflow so the demonstration can focus on relevant use cases.
